On the ground

Relief is people helping people.

The response is not one single moment. It is made up of rescue, care, safe spaces, and the steady work of rebuilding everyday life.

Immediate care

Families need support before recovery can begin.

Food, basic supplies, and human connection help families get through the first difficult days after a disaster.

Emergency response

Every minute matters when help is needed.

Local responders and relief teams work in difficult conditions to reach people, move them to safety, and deliver essential aid.

Community strength

Recovery is stronger when no one is left alone.

Communities carry one another through uncertainty. Your contribution helps make that shared effort more secure and more sustainable.

A safer tomorrow

Children deserve space to feel safe again.

Relief is also about protecting dignity, restoring routine, and giving children and families room to begin healing.

The work continues

Rebuilding takes patience, effort, and practical help.

Long after the first headlines fade, teams continue clearing damage, reaching households, and helping communities restore stability.

Shared hope

Small acts become visible change together.

Each contribution joins a wider network of people choosing to stand with Nepal and support a more hopeful recovery.
